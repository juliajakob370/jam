# 08 - Idle GIF and backlight dim

Blocked by: 03, 04

## What to build
When nothing plays, show an idle GIF for a few minutes. After that, dim the backlight. Wake the screen when music starts or when you tap it.

## Done when
- The idle GIF shows when playback stops.
- The backlight dims after the timeout. The timeout is set in the config file.
- A tap or new playback wakes the screen.
