# 10 - Case and v2 ideas

Blocked by: 01 (you need the real board to measure it)

## What to decide
- Case: buy a ready-made case, or print one. The library has a 3D printer.
- Check that the case fits the S028R board before you buy it.

## Later ideas
- External physical button on a spare GPIO pin.
- Battery power.
- A custom 3D-printed case.

## Done when
The board sits in a case on your desk and you can reach the USB port and the microSD slot.
