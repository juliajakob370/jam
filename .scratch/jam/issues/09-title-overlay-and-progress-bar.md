# 09 - Title overlay and progress bar

Blocked by: 03, 04

## What to build
Draw the song title and artist at the bottom of the GIF. Draw a thin progress bar under the text. Long titles must scroll or be cut with "...".

## Done when
- The text is easy to read on top of any GIF.
- The progress bar moves with the song.
- The overlay does not make the GIF flicker or slow down.
